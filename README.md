# Real Estate Time Series Forecasting

## Project Overview
This project analyzes historical real estate pricing data to identify trends and forecast future price movement. The goal is to support planning and decision-making by understanding long-term trends and short-term fluctuations in housing prices.

---

## Problem Statement
Real estate markets are influenced by long-term trends and cyclical behavior. This analysis explores historical pricing data to identify patterns and generate short-term forecasts that can inform planning and market assessment.

---

## Data
- Publicly available real estate pricing dataset  
- Historical price data recorded across multiple time periods  
- Dataset required cleaning and preparation prior to analysis  

---

## Tools & Methods
- **Python (Pandas, NumPy):** data preparation and transformation  
- **Time Series Analysis:** trend identification and seasonality review  
- **Forecasting Models:** applied to estimate near-term price movement  
- **Data Visualization:** charts illustrating historical trends and forecasts  

Key steps included:
- Cleaning and structuring time-based price data  
- Identifying long-term trends and cyclical behavior  
- Generating short-term forecasts based on historical patterns  

---

## Key Findings
- Historical pricing data showed clear long-term upward trends  
- Cyclical fluctuations were observed across time periods  
- Forecasts suggested continuation of established trend patterns in the near term  

---

## Impact / Use Case
The insights from this analysis can support:
- Market planning and investment assessment  
- Scenario planning and trend evaluation  
- Understanding market behavior over time  

This type of analysis is applicable to real estate, finance, and business analytics roles.
